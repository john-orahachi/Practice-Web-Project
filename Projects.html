About Writing Articles

It has been a burden on my heart since I became a Christian to share the Good News of Jesus Christ with men. Ever since I understood what the Holy Bible taught about the natural condition of man, since the fall of Adam, and also having seen with what superficiality men are dealt with by well meaning preachers and churchmen, my heart has often burned with a holy zeal to see to it that everyone did hear the Good News of Jesus, as it is the only means which God has ordained to save men by hearing of it.

I have since shared the Gospel of Jesus Christ with people within my circle, but also, and more recently, I have written some articles, by the help of God, which uses the incidents and key statements found in the Scriptures to explain the Gospel of Jesus Christ in simple terms.

By reading the articles, it is my earnest expectation that God will use them to minister to you, in drawing you to Himself thereby. Below are some of the available articles.

  • This Man Receiveth Sinners, Luke 15:2
  • If I do not wash you, John 13:8
  • My Gospel Burden

Children Ministry

In addition to writing articles, I also began a weekly program for children in a nearby neighborhood, which has been running for almost a year now. There, by the Grace of God, I have been able to gather children weekly, sit around the word of God.

Since November 2025, we've been examining the Gospel of John, as of August 30, 2026, we are in Chapter 5. We meet on Sundays at 4pm, pray, sing, and study the word of God together.

We have an Anthem we sing at the close of each meeting. It is popular Yoruba Hymn "Jesu ni Balogun oko" (Jesus is the Captain of the Ship). The writer is unknown. The hymn offers comfort to all who entrusts their lives into the hands of Jesus, with a calm assurance that the turbulence of life, like the raging sea, will not prevent the ship from reaching its desired haven. Jesus is able to keep us till the end of Life's journey.

My goal is not just to see that these children understand the Gospel early on in life, but that they also know and can defend the faith. And to achieve that ultimately, possessing a personal Bible becomes non-negotiable. So, I am embarking on a project to buy a Holy Bible for each one of them that doesn't have one right now.

Another thing I seek to achieve with these Children is to make sure that they learn to read, so that they can study the Scriptures themselves. To achieve this, I started recently an extension of the mission, where I'm attempting to teach them how to read as I best know how.
